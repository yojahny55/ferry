# Code of Conduct

Ferry follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Ferry to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors for
behavior they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@yojahny55](https://github.com/yojahny55), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/yojahny55/ferry/security/advisories/new) (it
reaches only the maintainer). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
